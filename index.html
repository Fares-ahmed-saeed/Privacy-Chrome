Privacy Policy for Tab Sleep

1. Overview

Tab Sleep is committed to protecting user privacy. This extension is designed solely to optimize your browser performance and reduce RAM usage by discarding inactive tabs. We operate under a strict zero-data-collection policy.

2. Data Storage & Usage

All data generated or used by the extension is processed and stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Tab Screenshots & States: Captured temporarily to show the tab preview and maintain scroll position. This data never leaves your browser.
  • User Preferences: Your customized whitelist of domains.
  • Analytics Data: Weekly RAM savings statistics shown on your dashboard.

None of this information is transmitted to external servers, third parties, or the developers.

3. Permissions Explained

To function effectively, Tab Sleep requires the following permissions, used strictly for local operations:

  • tabs: To detect inactive tabs, perform the discard action, and restore them upon click.
  • storage: To save your whitelist preferences and dashboard stats locally.
  • idle: To safely determine user inactivity before putting tabs to sleep.
  • alarms: To periodically check for inactive tabs in the background.
  • system.memory: To calculate and display your RAM savings data.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating this page. Your continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of any updates.
